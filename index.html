The Studio

Ten Years. One Obsession — Telling Your Story Right.

Gimbal Picture Creation began in a spare room with one camera, one gimbal, and a stubborn belief that wedding films didn't have to look staged. A decade later, we're a full studio of cinematographers, photographers, editors and drone pilots — but the belief hasn't changed.

We don't direct your wedding. We disappear into it — reading the room, chasing the light, and catching the moments that happen when nobody's posing. What comes back is a film and a gallery you'll actually want to watch again, not just store.
